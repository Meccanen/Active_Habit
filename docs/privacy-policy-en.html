Privacy Policy

Active Habit Tracker — Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Active Habit Tracker ("the App"), developed by Meccanen ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Android application. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Your Habits and Activity Data (stored only on your device)

The App lets you create habits, set goals, track your daily progress, and complete challenges. All of this data (your habits, logs, streaks, and settings) is stored locally on your device (for example in your device's local storage). We do not collect this data, send it to any server, or share it with any third party. There is no account system, and your data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

b) Advertising Identifiers

The App may display banner advertisements served through Google AdMob. To do so, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location, and other technical data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where you have provided consent (see Section 4, "Consent for Users in the EEA/UK").

c) Purchase Information

The App may offer optional in-app purchases (including an ad-free supporter feature) processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation of purchase status (purchased / not purchased) from Google Play, which is used locally on your device to unlock the relevant features.

d) Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not require or use your location, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying information, unless you voluntarily contact us (see Section 6). We do not operate our own analytics or crash-reporting backend.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and store your habits and daily activity locally on your device
  • To display and measure advertising through Google AdMob
  • To process and validate optional in-app purchases
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues (crash/error logs, where any, are handled by Google Play services)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the App will present a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before showing personalized advertising, in accordance with the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework and applicable GDPR requirements. You can change your consent choice at any time from the App's settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising served through the App is not configured to treat any user as a child, and we do not offer child-directed content.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • You can delete all of your habits and data at any time by clearing the App's data from your device settings, or by removing the App.
  • You can reset or limit ad tracking through your device's Google Settings → Ads menu.
  • You can change your personalized-advertising consent choice from the App's settings.
  • You can request information about, or deletion of, any data associated with your device by contacting us at the email below.

7. Data Retention

We do not operate our own backend database of user data. Your habits and daily activity data remain stored locally on your device for as long as you choose to keep them, and can be removed at any time. Any data processed to serve advertisements is handled by the respective third-party service (Google AdMob) according to its own retention policies.
