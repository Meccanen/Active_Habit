Privacy Policy

Meccanen Weather Assistant — Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Meccanen Weather Assistant ("the App"), developed by Meccanen ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Android application. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Location Data

The App requests access to your device's approximate or precise location (via ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) solely to determine your local weather conditions and forecast. Location data is sent to our weather data provider (Open-Meteo) to retrieve forecast information and is not stored on our servers or shared with any party other than the weather data provider required to fulfill the request. You may deny or revoke location permission at any time through your device settings; the App will fall back to manual city selection.

b) Advertising Identifiers

The App displays banner advertisements served through Google AdMob. To do so, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location, and other technical data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where you have provided consent (see Section 4, "Consent for Users in the EEA/UK").

c) Purchase Information

The App offers an optional one-time "Supporter Badge" ("Destekçi Rozeti") purchase processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation of purchase status (purchased / not purchased) from Google Play, which is used locally on your device to unlock supporter features.

d) Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying information, unless you voluntarily contact us (see Section 6).

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide accurate, location-based weather forecasts
  • To display and measure advertising through Google AdMob
  • To process and validate optional in-app purchases
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues (crash/error logs handled by Google Play services; we do not operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting backend of our own)

4. Consent for Users in the EEA / UK

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the App will present a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before showing personalized advertising, in accordance with the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework and applicable GDPR requirements. You can change your consent choice at any time from the App's settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising served through the App is not configured to treat any user as a child, and we do not offer child-directed content.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • You can disable location access at any time in your device's App permissions settings.
  • You can reset or limit ad tracking through your device's Google Settings → Ads menu.
  • You can request information about, or deletion of, any data associated with your device by contacting us at the email below.

7. Data Retention

We do not operate our own backend database of user data. Any data processed to serve weather forecasts or advertisements is handled transiently by the respective third-party service (Open-Meteo, Google AdMob) according to their own retention policies.
